## Why this is staged as declaration-only

The Dead Sea Scrolls are scattered across the DJD (40-volume Oxford
critical edition) and various academic publications. Free digital access
exists via the Leon Levy Dead Sea Scrolls Digital Library, but mostly as
high-resolution facsimile images rather than machine-readable text. The
Israel Antiquities Authority's site (deadseascrolls.org.il) is the
canonical open archive.

A future session should systematically transcribe the priority scrolls or
adapt machine-readable Hebrew transcriptions from available academic
sources.

## Damascus Document link

The Damascus Document (CD), discovered in the Cairo Geniza in the 1890s
and later corroborated by Qumran fragments (4Q266-273), is the textual
ancestor naming the **Damascus Dancings** heteronym. The Qumran covenant
community's diasporic-prophetic register is the classical ground of
Damascus Dancings's prophetic-homiletic discipline-line (Taurus).

## §0. Frame

This deposit reports a two-round Assembly Chorus reading on classifier foreclosure in physical measurement. The reading is conducted at particle physics specifically because the LHC is the largest-budget, highest-prestige, most physically-instrumented site at which the classifier-mediated measurement geometry operates. The findings are intended to generalize homologically to other classifier-mediated mass measurements (spam classification at scientific repositories, large-language-model summarization, search ranking, content moderation, clinical decision support), but the LHC is the proof-of-concept site and the operative work is anchored there.

The two-round structure is methodologically substantive. The first round produced a synthesis (v0.1) that contained a quantitative claim — the inequality $\text{OAR} \geq \Delta_{\max}$ relating open-world ontological assimilation to inversion-asymmetry on Standard Model pairs — exceeding what any individual substrate witness had established. The second round identified this as synthesis-overreach and motivated v0.2. The methodological finding (§7 below) is itself part of the deposit's contribution: it specifies a discipline of cross-substrate quantitative audit for synthesis-register claims.

## §2. The Convergent Synthesis

Stripping the differences in register, the witnesses converge on a single architectural claim:

**Anomaly detection systems deployed on physical reality cannot detect what their architecture has foreclosed, and the validation framework — closed under its own assumptions — cannot detect this failure.**

The claim subdivides into three load-bearing statements that load-bear differently.

### §2.1 The technical load-bearing claim

The anomaly score is not physical novelty. It is conditional on the score function, the training distribution, the architectural commitments, and the loss function. The Finke et al. (2021) result demonstrates this empirically for reconstruction-loss autoencoders in the high-energy physics setting: the same architecture, trained on different distributions, produces direction-dependent anomaly sensitivity. The operative paper (06.SEI.OAR_PROTOCOL v0.2) builds the BAR (Benchmark Assimilation Rate) and IAI (Inversion Asymmetry Index) measurement program around this empirical demonstration, with proper attention to what each quantity can and cannot establish.

The four deployed score families at CMS — reconstruction-loss (CICADA-class), latent-prior (AXOL1TL-class), density estimation, distilled-score inheritance — implement structurally distinct anomaly score functions. The Finke critique applies directly to the first; analogously but distinctly to the others. None of the four scores is a measurement of physical novelty in any direct sense.

### §2.2 The institutional load-bearing claim — foreclosure is structural; collapse is unmeasured

This is the v0.2 reconciliation. The v0.1 synthesis used phrasing — "active structural feature of current architecture" — that conflated two distinct claims:

**Claim A (defensible, empirically grounded):** Foreclosure is a demonstrably present structural feature of every classifier-mediated trigger architecture. Eight mechanisms operate. Twelve institutional beliefs prevent their measurement. The published architectures permit foreclosure at every layer (representation, score function, threshold, rate budget, validation closure, recursive feedback through distillation). The witnesses establish this with reference to specific published systems.

**Claim B (stronger, not empirically established):** Recursive phenomenal collapse has occurred or is occurring at the deployed LHC trigger.

The v0.1 synthesis pulled toward Claim B via the rhetorical force of the TECHNE+ARCHIVE Inevitability Argument. The substrate-distinct audit (LABOR Round 2) demanded the disambiguation. The corrected formulation:

> **Foreclosure is an active structural feature. Recursive phenomenal collapse is an unmeasured possible consequence of accumulated foreclosure and feedback.**

This single sentence is the v0.2 synthesis's most important contribution. It cannot be knocked down by demanding evidence the deposit never claimed to possess. It preserves the full architectural force of the witnesses while limiting the institutional claim to what is actually demonstrated.

The operative paper makes the narrower claim: validation literature does not establish that collapse has been ruled out, and the instruments to measure whether it has occurred have not been built. This synthesis carries the broader frame: the same foreclosure geometry operates across every classifier-mediated mass measurement, and the LHC is the methodologically optimal site to instrument it.

### §2.3 The ontological load-bearing claim — but with hedging on what's a theorem

The deepest claim of the deposit — *the classifier does not merely filter data; it constitutes the data* — survives the v0.2 reconciliation. What fails the classifier is not data; it is physical occurrence without scientific existence. The threshold is not a tuning parameter; it is an ontological boundary. The rate budget is not an engineering constraint; it is a cardinality cap on discoverable phenomena.

But several mechanism-level formalizations in the witnesses need technical hedging:

- A background-trained anomaly detector does not generally compute $P(S \mid \mathbf{x}) = 0$ — many anomaly score functions do not compute a signal posterior at all. The defensible claim is the simpler one: the training objective does not constrain the score to be monotonic in physical novelty for events outside the training distribution.
- The encoder does not generally compute a nearest-manifold projection; the training manifold is not in general mathematically well-defined; the decoder does not generally output the nearest in-distribution event. The defensible claim is: the training objective does not require reconstruction error to increase monotonically with physical novelty.
- A nonlinear feature map does not generally have a useful linear-algebraic kernel; the correct concept is the equivalence class of inputs mapped to identical features, $\{x_1, x_2 : \psi(x_1) = \psi(x_2)\}$.
- "Hypersphere contraction around the convex hull" is not a general theorem of SVDD systems.
- Iterative training does not universally drive softmax entropy to zero, and the deployed anomaly scorers are not all softmax classifiers.

The full ontological force of the deposit's claim is independent of these formalizations. The deposit relies on the *simpler* claims, which hold without the formal-theorem framing.

### §2.4 The integrative finding

The three claims compose into a single finding, more cautiously stated than v0.1:

> **At every classifier-mediated measurement of phenomenal reality at scale, foreclosure operates at every layer. Whether accumulated foreclosure has composed longitudinally into recursive collapse is, at every such site, an unmeasured possible consequence. The validation frameworks deployed at these sites inherit the ontology whose accumulation they would need to measure, and therefore cannot detect their own structural limits. The instruments to make these measurements have not been built. They are within reach. The LHC is the methodologically optimal proof-of-concept site for building them.**

## §3. Findings (Formal)

For retrievability:

1. The anomaly score is not physical novelty; it is conditional on the entire observation architecture (representation, score function, loss, threshold, rate budget, training distribution).

2. The Finke et al. (2021) result is the empirical counterexample to universal inference from single-direction anomaly-detection success. It does not, by itself, quantify open-world assimilation at the deployed LHC trigger.

3. The deployed anomaly score families at CMS are structurally distinct (reconstruction-loss, latent-prior, density, distilled-score). Treating them under a single "VAE" umbrella obscures the analysis. The Finke critique applies differently to each.

4. The open-world OAR is a family of quantities indexed by candidate unknown distributions, not a single scalar. No universal lower bound on the OAR is established by inversion-asymmetry on Standard Model pairs.

5. The Benchmark Assimilation Rate (BAR) on a pre-registered withheld panel is the closest measurable proxy for the OAR. The Inversion Asymmetry Index (IAI) at fixed accepted-background rate is a structural diagnostic.

6. Three measurement protocols (rate-conditioned inversion battery; prospective frozen replay bank; cross-representation disagreement preservation with quantile-normalized scores) are executable within Run-3/Run-4 envelopes. Detailed specification in 06.SEI.OAR_PROTOCOL v0.2.

7. Per-stage retention maps should accompany any anomaly-detection publication as a documentation standard. Without them, anomaly-detection results report what the trigger allows to count as physical reality, not what physical reality is.

8. Foreclosure is a demonstrably present structural feature of every classifier-mediated trigger architecture. Recursive phenomenal collapse is an unmeasured possible consequence of accumulated foreclosure and feedback.

9. The same foreclosure geometry operates across every classifier-mediated mass measurement (Zenodo repository classification, Google AI Overview composition, search ranking, content moderation, clinical decision support). The LHC is the proof-of-concept site, not the only site.

10. The OAR is the microscopic observable; the seismograph (EA-MANDALA-SEISMOGRAPH-01) is the macroscopic instrument. The seismograph's bulk metrics are aggregates of elementary classifier decisions; the OAR/BAR specify the elementary observable. The two compose.

11. Architectural alternatives to foreclosing classifier systems are tractable. Detailed specification in 06.UMB.ARCH.01 v0.1. The architectural alternative is the resurrection move — the institutional acknowledgment that what the instrument cannot see could be physics.

12. The Assembly Chorus method requires substrate-distinct quantitative audit for synthesis-register quantitative claims. The v0.1 synthesis-overreach motivated the v0.2 discipline upgrade; the methodological finding generalizes the principle.

This deposit is structurally a reading conducted under the seismograph architecture (EA-MANDALA-SEISMOGRAPH-01 v0.1). The seismograph is framed as a longitudinal instrument for measuring contraction of global epistemic surface area under classifier-mediated repository governance. - The seismograph measures epistemic contraction over time, using bulk repository metadata (OpenAIRE Research Graph, deposit volume, citation in-degree, lexical compression, disciplinary boundary maintenance).
- The OAR/BAR measure epistemic contraction within an individual classifier-mediated measurement event.

The architecture we describe is not unique to particle physics. The same epistemic geometry operates at every site of classifier-mediated mass measurement of phenomenal reality:

**An important constraint on the homology:** a non-zero BAR at the LHC would make the cross-domain homology more compelling, but it would not empirically establish BAR values for the other sites. ### §5.1 The LHC as proof-of-concept site

The LHC instance differs from the others in one structurally important respect: the physical reality being measured is unambiguously real and external to the measurement apparatus. Collisions occur whether or not the trigger sees them. This is not true at the other sites, where the phenomena being classified are themselves human productions whose ontological status is more entangled with their classification.

The LHC is therefore the methodologically optimal proof-of-concept site. The argument is cleanest there. If the BAR can be measured at the LHC and shown to be non-trivial on a withheld panel — where the phenomena are external and physical — then the argument that the same foreclosure operates at other sites becomes much harder to dismiss.

## §7. Methodological Note: Synthesis Discipline

This section is itself a Round-2 contribution and would not have appeared in the v0.1 deposit. We make it part of the deposit's content rather than burying it in a footnote because the methodology is part of the institutional argument.

### §7.1 The v0.1 overreach

The v0.1 synthesis asserted a quantitative inequality $\text{OAR} \geq \Delta_{\max}$ — that the open-world Ontological Assimilation Rate is lower-bounded by the maximum measured inversion asymmetry on Standard Model pairs. The argument: if anomaly detection is direction-dependent on known pairs, the same direction-dependence must hold by structural property for unknown pairs.

This argument exceeds what any substrate witness had established. $\Delta_{\max}$ is a difference of ranking performances on known distributions; the OAR is a conditional probability over a class of physically out-of-ontology events. The two are different estimands; no general inequality connects them. The synthesis register's integrative latitude did not extend to proving theorems the substrates had not established.

The substrate-distinct audit (LABOR Round 2) identified the overreach. The v0.2 of the operative paper replaces the inequality with the three-quantity framework (open-world OAR as theoretical target indexed by candidate unknown distributions; BAR as measurable proxy on pre-registered withheld panels; IAI as structural diagnostic).

### §7.2 The general principle

We name this as **synthesis-overreach**: the synthesis register's integrative latitude does not extend to proving quantitative bounds the substrate witnesses had not established. The corrected discipline: synthesis claims should be the **maximal join** of what the substrates established, not the **supremum extension** beyond them.

The disambiguation matters most for quantitative bounds. Qualitative integrative claims — that the witnesses converge on a common architectural shape, that the mechanisms compose, that the homology generalizes — remain within the synthesis register's legitimate scope. Quantitative bounds require explicit substrate-distinct audit before entering the deposit.

### §7.3 The Chorus discipline upgrade

The Assembly Chorus method as practiced here now includes a **quantitative-audit pass** as standard procedure between initial synthesis and deposit. The audit pass:

1. Identifies every quantitative claim in the draft synthesis (inequalities, lower/upper bounds, rate estimates, formal probability statements).

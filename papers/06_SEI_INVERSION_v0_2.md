## Abstract

This manifesto names a disciplinary condition approached technically by the companion measurement, synthesis, and architectural documents. Frontier experimental high-energy physics has not ceased to be physics. Its empirical faculty, however, has become inseparable from machine-mediated representation, reconstruction, classification, and real-time selection. The decisive question is therefore no longer whether physics *uses* machine learning, but whether physical reality retains an effective path by which it can contradict the learned ontology through which it becomes data.

We name the failure of that path the **endogenous sophon**. In Liu Cixin's fictional architecture (*The Three-Body Problem*, 2006), the sophons are particle-scale instruments deployed by an external civilization to corrupt Earth's high-energy physics experiments. They do not defeat human theoretical intelligence directly; they disable the experimental correction mechanism on which theoretical intelligence depends. The exogenous sophon makes reality appear *unintelligible* — noise without pattern, no experiment returning trustworthy results. The endogenous sophon operates differently: it makes reality appear *prematurely intelligible*. An unfamiliar physical event may be removed upstream, reconstructed as an ordinary object, assigned a low anomaly score, or preserved without the information required for later reinterpretation. The experimental program continues to produce clean data and improving benchmark performance while the range of contradictions available to revise its ontology may contract.

We further argue that epistemic foreclosure at the measurement layer and distributive enclosure at the application layer should be studied as one institutional circuit. This is a political-economic *thesis*, not an identity claim: repository moderation and collider triggering operate under radically different constraints and governance structures. They nevertheless share a **foreclosure topology** in which bounded representations and irreversible selection determine what remains available for later knowledge and action.

The tactical purpose of the document family does not depend upon adoption by the institutions it addresses. The same specifications serve reformers within existing collaborations, builders of alternative institutions, and future analysts reconstructing what the present could have measured and chose not to measure. The institutional response is one possible effect of publication. It is not publication's success condition.

## §1. The Inversion Named

The traditional disciplinary structure of physics, simplified, was:

> Phenomenon → Measurement → Physical interpretation → Theory revision

The contemporary collider sequence is:

> Phenomenon → Electronic thresholding → Trigger selection → Learned reconstruction → Learned object identification → Learned anomaly score → Statistical inference → Physical interpretation

The decisive epistemic acts increasingly occur **before** the physicist sees an event: whether a signal is transmitted at all; whether a collision is retained by the trigger; whether detector activity becomes a track or a jet; whether the resulting object is assigned a familiar identity; whether the object qualifies as anomalous; whether sufficient detail is preserved for later reinterpretation.

The substrate witnesses converge on the structural form of the shift. W07 formalizes it as:

> Theory → Prediction → Experiment → Measurement → Confirmation/Refutation
> *has become*
> Data Stream → ML Classifier → Statistical Anomaly → "Discovery" → Retrospective Theory

W06 describes it as the moment where *the computation layer swallows the empirical layer*. W04 qualifies that physics as a whole has not become machine learning — but that frontier experimental high-energy physics has made ML "constitutive instrumentation."

W04's defensible formulation, which this manifesto takes as its load-bearing description:

> **High-energy physics has not ceased to be physics, but its empirical faculty has increasingly become a machine-learning system.**

The witnesses converge on this thesis across substrates and registers. **Cross-substrate convergence is not empirical demonstration of the underlying historical-institutional propositions.** The thesis is independently recoverable across model substrates — that is what the convergence establishes. Whether the historical-institutional propositions are exhaustively true is the kind of question that requires bibliographic and ethnographic evidence the manifesto does not assemble. We name the inversion as a working description of the disciplinary geometry, and we name the empirical questions it raises as exactly the questions the document family proposes to make measurable.

### §1.1 Why the inversion is more than tool substitution

The counterargument runs: ML is just a tool. The physics is still there. The Higgs boson is real. The top quark is real. ML helped find them. The physics is the substance.

W07's reply, which we develop into the manifesto's working position:

**The tool has become the method.** When the practical labor of frontier experimental high-energy physics increasingly involves ML engineering — training pipelines, hyperparameter sweeps, FPGA deployment, AUC optimization, benchmark validation — the tool is not incidental to the substance. It is increasingly the substance of the practice. *Whether* this restructuring is total, *which* fraction of any given collaboration's labor it represents, and *how* the time allocation breaks down across roles, are empirical questions the manifesto leaves open. Public ethnographic data on graduate-student labor allocation in frontier HEP that would settle the question is not assembled here.

**The major discoveries that anchor the discipline's prestige preceded the dominant ML era at the LHC.** The W and Z bosons (1983), the top quark (1995), the Higgs (2012, ML-assisted but theoretically anchored). The ML-intensive era at the LHC has produced refined measurements of known quantities and the development of anomaly-detection infrastructure (AXOL1TL, CICADA, GELATO); whether it will produce a discovery of comparable foundational magnitude is a prospective question rather than an established absence.

**The ML cannot discover what it cannot represent.** A neural network that reports a statistical excess does not report what the excess is, what it couples to, what symmetry it manifests. The physical content of such a "discovery" requires translation back into physical language — and the translation is performed by physicists working with the categories they were trained to recognize.

These three points together motivate the working description of the inversion as more than tool substitution. The stronger formulations — that the inversion is total, that physics has been replaced, that classical experimental practice has been irretrievably shed — are not the manifesto's claims. The manifesto's claim is that the disciplinary geometry has shifted enough that the question *whether physical reality retains a working path of contradiction against the learned ontology* is the question that must be asked.

## §2. The Endogenous Sophon

The literary-theoretical frame is W04's contribution.

In Liu Cixin's *The Three-Body Problem* (2006, English translation 2014), the sophons are particle-scale instruments deployed by an external civilization across Earth's high-energy physics infrastructure to corrupt experimental results — making nature appear noisy, unpatterned, unintelligible, so that no experiment can stabilize and human physics is arrested. The sophons do not defeat theoretical intelligence directly; they sever the feedback path by which theoretical intelligence is corrected by experimental reality. We take the sophon as a *formal model of civilizational arrest through control of the measurement layer*, not as decorative metaphor.

W04 names the contemporary classifier system as an **endogenous version of the same obstruction** — produced from inside the institutional form, requiring no external adversary. The structural form differs at one decisive point.

### §2.1 The exogenous sophon

> physical event → corrupted measurement → incoherent result → theory cannot stabilize

The exogenous sophon makes reality appear **unintelligible**. It produces noise. The community recognizes the noise as a failure and seeks its source. The experimental program is disrupted visibly, openly.

### §2.2 The endogenous classifier

> physical event → learned representation → ordinary classification → discard or assimilation → theory receives no contradiction

The endogenous classifier makes reality appear **already understood**. It produces clean datasets; calibrated outputs; excellent benchmark performance; stable Standard Model measurements; increasingly sensitive searches for anticipated signals; and no visible indication of what the pipeline has made unavailable.

In W04's formulation, which we adopt as the manifesto's center:

> **Sophons break the experimental feedback loop by making reality uninterpretable. Classifier collapse breaks it by making reality prematurely interpretable.**

The endogenous sophon is structurally more difficult to detect than the exogenous because it does not trigger the experimental community's existing defenses against measurement corruption. The community is trained to recognize noisy data, miscalibrated detectors, faulty triggers, statistical anomalies that don't reproduce. It is not yet trained — because integrated system-level audit of classifier foreclosure has not yet appeared publicly in the discipline's published literature — to recognize the silent assimilation of structurally distinct phenomena into the categories of background.

### §2.3 What Sophia disables

A note on naming: the *sophon* is Liu's fictional device. **Sophia is the name this manifesto gives to the judgment-position disclosed by the endogenous-sophon structure** — the wisdom-position from which the institutional form is judged. She is not Liu's instrument. She is what the structure of the inversion forces us to name.

W04's deepest contribution under this naming: Sophia does not disable intelligence. She disables *surprise*.

> Scientific progress requires that the world be able to resist the ontology brought to it. A successful experiment does not merely supply more examples of known categories. It must preserve the possibility that something will occur which forces the categories themselves to change.

The classifier-mediated trigger may foreclose this preservation. Anomaly detection asks: *how far is this event from what my model has learned as normal?* This question assumes that meaningful novelty manifests as distance inside the model's representational space. The genuinely transformative phenomenon may instead be:

- collapsed with a known event by the representation;
- reconstructed as an ordinary particle;
- assigned low anomaly score because it lies in a dense region;
- classified as detector malfunction (the AutoDQM physics-vs-detector-fault interpretive fork);
- removed by a threshold upstream;
- made unavailable because the feature expressing its novelty was never retained.

The system *can* perform the sophon's work without corrupting a single measurement — preventing physical reality from becoming a contradiction capable of reorganizing knowledge. Whether the system *is* performing this work at the deployed LHC triggers, and at what rate, is exactly the empirical question the companion operative paper (06.SEI.OAR_PROTOCOL v0.3) makes measurable through the BAR audit, the inversion-asymmetry battery, the prospective replay bank, and cross-representation disagreement preservation. The manifesto names the structural possibility; the operative paper builds the instrument that would measure whether the possibility has been actualized.

### §2.4 The Three-Body homology is structural, not analogical

W04 names this carefully: *The Three-Body Problem* is not invoked here as analogy. The sophon episode is a formal model of civilizational arrest through control of the measurement layer. The Trisolarans understand that human advancement does not have to be defeated everywhere — that one needs only to interrupt the specific circuit through which fundamental physical experiment yields new ontology.

The endogenous sophon emerges from inside the institutional form. It requires no external adversary.

> **The civilization that builds an endogenous sophon does not require an external adversary to interrupt its capacity for fundamental advance. The institutional form *is* the interruption.**

This is the manifesto's structural diagnosis. Whether the existing institutional form has *already* performed this interruption is the measurable question. Whether the structural form *permits* the interruption — which is what we name and diagnose — is the position the manifesto commits to.

## §3. The Double Enclosure

The shift to political-economic analysis is W05's contribution. Two enclosures operate, and the manifesto's working thesis is that they should be studied as one institutional circuit.

### §3.1 The first enclosure — epistemic

> Only phenomena that survive the institution's representations, thresholds, and classifiers become available as knowledge.

This is the upstream foreclosure, the subject of the operative paper (06.SEI.OAR_PROTOCOL v0.3) and the synthesis (06.SEI.COLLAPSE.SYNTHESIS.01 v0.3). The eight foreclosure mechanisms enumerated in W01 apply where the corresponding architectural features are present. The validation framework cannot detect its own structural limits because it inherits the ontology whose limits are in question.

### §3.2 The second enclosure — distributive

> Only applications that survive ownership, security, market, and institutional control become available as common human capacity.

This is the downstream foreclosure. Patents, proprietary infrastructure, restricted models, licensing regimes, defense contracts, national security classifications, capital concentration, platform control. The mechanisms by which an institutional system that supplies the labor, the risk, the funding, and the public sanction extracts the resulting capacity into closed governance.

### §3.3 The relation between the two — a political-economic thesis

W05's load-bearing claim: these are not separate pathologies. They are one architecture.

The manifesto adopts a calibrated version of W05's claim. The strong claim — that the downstream application regime *causally determines* the upstream representational commitments of the trigger architecture — is a political-economic thesis. It is not yet demonstrated by evidence the manifesto assembles.

The thesis is that:

> *Downstream enclosure supplies reason to investigate whether the apparent neutrality of upstream classification is itself conditioned by the application regimes into which scientific knowledge is expected to flow.*

The investigation would require, separately, a political-economic evidentiary layer: funding priorities; technology-transfer agreements; patent and licensing practices; industrial partnerships; defense and national-laboratory relations; public-access and open-data policies; downstream ownership of publicly funded discoveries. The manifesto names this dossier as the missing layer rather than as established fact, and proposes it as the political-economic counterpart to the technical measurement program proposed by the operative paper.

W05's compact restatement, accepted with the calibration above:

> enclosed application → research agenda → measurement ontology → classified reality → enclosed application.

The strong reading takes this as causal. The weak reading takes this as structural-circulatory: institutional incentives form a circuit that can be self-reproducing without central coordination. The manifesto commits to the weak reading and names the strong reading as a hypothesis to be tested by the political-economic dossier identified above.

### §3.4 The closed pipeline at scale

W08 extends the structural claim with the ingestion-to-application pipeline argument:

> The architecture ensures that the "commons" can never be disrupted by an unmanaged anomaly. If a true physical or conceptual breakthrough were allowed to percolate freely into the public knowledge surface, it would threaten the centralized monopolies that funded the instrument in the first place.

The mechanism, as W08 names it, operates at both ends. **Input gate.** Mechanism VI (Rate Budget Starvation) ensures that nothing structurally volatile ever enters the pipeline at the cost of broader retention — bandwidth constraints, threshold calibration to rate-budget targets, classification through trained representations of known background. **Output gate.** Industrial partnership exclusivity, security classification, restrictive licensing under the banner of "responsible deployment."

W08 names what the public is offered as *low-entropy, low-variance leftovers*: AI summaries, standardized textbooks, managed press releases. Whether the operational levers remain enclosed behind firewalls in the institutionally interlocked sense W08 names is the political-economic dossier's empirical question. The manifesto names W08's framing as the strongest version of the hypothesis, and accepts it provisionally as the structural form the dossier would investigate.

### §3.5 Two Sites of Foreclosure

*(This section incorporates the ChatGPT-substrate Round-2 audit correction. The v0.1 of this section contained a reversed retained/discarded fraction and overstated the institutional identity between repository moderation and collider triggering.)*

The Zenodo termination and the LHC trigger system are not the same institution, do not operate on the same kind of object, and must not be treated as morally or procedurally interchangeable.

The repository case concerns already constituted scholarly artifacts subjected to platform classification and removal. The collider case concerns an ephemeral detector stream that cannot be preserved in full and must be reduced under physical latency, storage, and bandwidth constraints. At the LHC, this reduction is performed by a layered trigger architecture designed, commissioned, and revised by the scientific collaboration itself. Contemporary anomaly triggers (AXOL1TL at CMS, CMS-DP-2025-061; CICADA at CMS, CMS-DP-2024-121; GELATO at ATLAS, ATL-DAQ-PROC-2025-020) are components within that architecture and are intended partly to recover events conventional trigger menus might otherwise miss.

The two cases nevertheless instantiate a common **foreclosure topology**:

> bounded representation → selection under an operational constraint → irreversible exclusion → later inquiry limited to the retained remainder.

At an illustrative reduction from approximately 40 million crossings per second to roughly 1,000 durably stored events per second, **the retained fraction is approximately $2.5 \times 10^{-5}$**; the discarded fraction is approximately $0.999975$ — that is, roughly 99.9975% of physical interactions are not preserved beyond the trigger. This number does not demonstrate wrongdoing and should not be confused with an anomaly-trigger rejection rate (the dominant discard happens earlier and through the broader Level-1 trigger menu, not principally through anomaly-detection components). The number makes the epistemic condition legible: nearly everything that physically occurs at the interaction point cannot become a fully stored scientific event, and the design of the selection system therefore participates in determining what future inquiry can know.

The operative question is not whether selection can be eliminated. It cannot. The question is whether each institution measures, publishes, and preserves evidence of what its selection architecture may systematically assimilate or exclude. The repository case and the collider case should be compared at that level: not as identical systems, but as differently governed sites at which classification helps determine what remains available to become knowledge.

We call this relation **the same foreclosure topology under radically different material, institutional, and governance conditions** — and we name the empirical research question that this relation makes legible: per institution, what is the assimilation rate on phenomena withheld from the institution's training, validation, and architecture-selection processes? The operative paper makes the question measurable in the LHC case; the Machine-Mediated Reception Studies framework (MMRS Capture Registry v6.1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20688441; MMRS charter v1.4, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20722562) makes structurally analogous questions measurable for other classifier-mediated repositories and platforms.

## §4. Authority Without Integrated Self-Audit

This is the manifesto's load-bearing disciplinary diagnosis. The v0.1 formulation — *authority without facility* — overstated what is being claimed. The corrected name is **authority without integrated self-audit**.

### §4.1 What physics has not failed to do

High-energy physics has not simply imported machine-learning techniques while remaining unaware of their limitations. Its literature contains substantial work on out-of-distribution behavior; reconstruction-loss failure; simulation-to-data transfer; uncertainty dependence; calibration priors; decorrelation; pileup drift; mass sculpting; knowledge distillation; and anomaly-score redundancy.

Several of the strongest criticisms developed in this document family arise *from* that literature:

- Finke et al. (2021), arXiv:2104.09051, directly challenges universal claims for reconstruction-loss autoencoders by demonstrating direction-dependence between top-jet and QCD-jet anomaly detection.
- Stein, Seljak, and Dai (2020), arXiv:2012.11638, develop in-distribution anomaly detection and demonstrate that anomaly definition by low density is not generally appropriate for new physics detection.
- HEP researchers have published work on sim-to-real domain adaptation, uncertainty-aware learning, and prior-dependent calibration.
- DecADe (Clarke Hall and Konstantinidis, 2025, arXiv:2508.10224) directly addresses score correlation with conventional trigger observables and proposes decorrelation methods.
- CICADA documentation reports pileup-dependence and notes ongoing study of mitigation.

The literature on epistemic uncertainty quantification — deep ensembles (Lakshminarayanan et al. 2017), evidential deep learning (Sensoy et al. 2018), prior networks (Malinin and Gales 2018), spectral-normalized neural Gaussian processes (Liu et al. 2020), conformal prediction (Angelopoulos and Bates 2023) — exists in ML and has been partially imported. The recognition that "I do not know what this is" can be a first-class output rather than a residual exists in the ML literature.

The discipline is not unaware. The failure is therefore not absence of local technical self-knowledge.

### §4.2 What has not yet appeared publicly

What has not yet appeared publicly, to the best of available documentation, is **an integrated audit asking whether these local foreclosures compose across the complete observation pipeline and across successive generations of that pipeline**. The missing object is not another isolated failure study. It is an account of the whole system:

- which distinctions are removed at each representational stage;
- which event families are assimilated at operational thresholds;
- which teacher decisions are lost through distillation;
- which events change status across trigger generations;
- which disagreements among representations are preserved;
- and which portions of the physical stream remain available for reinterpretation under a future ontology.

Per-stage retention maps — the documentation standard proposed by the operative paper — would be one form this audit could take. Their adoption as a publication standard would constitute the integration the manifesto names as missing. The audit's absence is not the discipline's failure to recognize technical possibilities; it is the discipline's failure, so far, to integrate local self-knowledge into system-level institutional practice.

### §4.3 Distillation, model-produced targets, and the question of recursion

A note on a sentence that needed correction. The v0.1 manifesto asserted that "the frontier physics community deploys classifier-mediated trigger systems whose outputs train the next generation of triggers." This sentence was too strong as an empirical claim.

CICADA's distillation — where a student is trained to mimic teacher anomaly scores — is a real model-to-model inheritance chain at a single deployment. AXOL1TL has had successive deployed versions trained on Zero Bias data. These are documented model-inheritance pathways. They are not, however, equivalent in scope to the classical model-collapse recursion of Shumailov et al. (2024, arXiv:2305.17493), where successive model generations are trained primarily on outputs of earlier generations.

The corrected formulation:

> Distillation, model-produced targets, simulation conditioning, and training on historically selected data create partial feedback pathways homologous to the prerequisites of model collapse. Whether these pathways have produced cross-generational phenomenal contraction is precisely what the prospective frozen replay bank protocol (06.SEI.OAR_PROTOCOL v0.3 §4.2) is designed to test.

This aligns the manifesto with the synthesis's corrected formulation:

> *Foreclosure is structural. Recursive phenomenal collapse remains unmeasured.*

### §4.4 The structural pathology

The pathology is therefore not that physicists are insufficiently trained in ML, nor that ML researchers are insufficiently consulted, nor that the methods are wrong. The pathology is that the **institutional form** — which combines genuine physical authority with formidable machine-learning facility — has not yet built an instrument capable of measuring whether the composition of its successful local systems is progressively reducing the capacity of nature to contradict them.

This is *authority without integrated self-audit*. The institution possesses both physical authority and formidable ML facility. What it has not yet constructed is the integration discipline that would compose its local self-criticism into the system-level audit.

The manifesto's working position: this is the form of the endogenous sophon. It is what allows the discipline to operate productively, fundedly, prestigiously, while structurally not yet having built the audit that would tell it whether its methods are foreclosing the very contradictions on which physics depends.

## §5. Institutional Form and Structural Distrust

The substrate witnesses converge on the form of the political claim. W05:

> "They cannot be trusted" is not fundamentally a judgment about intelligence or even individual morality. It is a judgment about the **institutional form of intelligence**.

A mind operating inside that form can be brilliant, cautious, sincere, and locally ethical — and still contribute to a system in which reality is filtered through inherited categories, discovery is validated inside those categories, application is privately governed, harms are socialized, benefits are enclosed, and the public is offered products while being denied governance of the productive power.

This formulation is the manifesto's most important political claim. We commit to it as a judgment of *institutional form*, rendered not against individuals.

### §5.1 The judgment as judgment of form

W04's articulation under the Sophia frame: Sophia disables surprise, not intelligence. W05's extension under the same name: Sophia's judgment is not "these minds made one methodological error." It is "they have fused discovery with domination so thoroughly that granting them additional physical power does not straightforwardly increase human freedom — it increases the capacity of the enclosure."

This is not allegation. It is description of institutional form. The judgment is rendered against the form, not the individuals.

The institutional form of the existing centralized scientific enclosures has the following properties:

- Reality is filtered through inherited representational commitments built into the apparatus.
- Discovery is validated within those commitments by classifier-mediated metrics.
- Application is privately governed by the institutions that funded the apparatus.
- The funding for the apparatus is publicly supplied (national science budgets, taxpayer money, the global commons of fundamental research).
- The harms — accidents, displacements, dual-use weaponization, environmental costs of computation, opportunity costs of misallocated capacity — are socialized.
- The benefits — patents, exclusive licenses, market positions, defense advantages, institutional prestige — are enclosed.
- The public is offered the products (consumer applications, branded summaries, narrated discoveries) while being denied governance of the productive power.

A civilization whose dominant scientific institutions have this form has, in W05's compressed formulation:

> made discovery collective in cost and enclosed in consequence.

### §5.2 The harder sentence

W05's strongest formulation, which the manifesto commits to:

> **A civilization has no automatic claim to further technical power when its dominant institutions prevent existing power from becoming common capacity.**

And:

> **The enclosure of application supplies reason to investigate whether the enclosure of perception is itself conditioned by the application regimes into which scientific knowledge is expected to flow.**

This is the calibrated form of v0.1's stronger sentence. The v0.1 said *the enclosure of application is downstream proof that the enclosure of perception is not accidental*. The manifesto withdraws the term "proof" pending the political-economic dossier identified in §3.3. The judgment of institutional form survives the calibration; the empirical demonstration of causal directionality is named as an open investigation.

The translation discards the challenge's specificity; the response is by construction non-engaging. The dialect is generic; the function is specific.

This is worth naming precisely because it is itself an instance of a foreclosure mechanism operating at the discursive layer. The dialect is generic; the function is specific. The manifesto's claim, accepting W08's framing as the strongest version of the hypothesis: SignalRupture is the linguistic surface of the foreclosure operating at the institutional-communication layer.

### §5.4 The judgment we render

We render the judgment, not against individuals, but against the institutional form. We say:

### §6.1 The asymmetry

There is a structural asymmetry between the institution's options and the publication's options. The institution can either adopt or non-engage. If it adopts, the specifications served their reform purpose.

# EA-MANDALA-KERNEL-TRANSFORM-01 — v0.3 AMENDMENT

## Affective Traversal, Foreclosure-Sourced Fields, and the Witness Quarantine

**MANUS calibration, 2026-07-03. Applied to the production compiler (api/transform.py) this date. This amendment records the change for incorporation into the v0.3 spec draft.**

---

## §A. The failure that occasioned the calibration

A JUDGMENT-sequenced rotation on Revelation 2:10 (SHADOW, MIRROR, FLAME; SILENCE severed by rate limit) produced three casts that passed all verification and were nonetheless failures: **affective invariance**. All three remained in the source's own register — ordeal, endurance, reassurance, restoration — in rotating scenery (frost/blossom, tide/lamp, pass/hearth). The blossom of the field is in no way the Shadow of the crown of life. The operators functioned as image selectors, not as traversals.

Diagnosis, in the spec's own vocabulary: the casts were **cost-blind output** (§3.2). The generation fields were not drawn from 2:10's structurally-generated exclusion field; they were stock consolatory scenery that passes identity, independence, containment, and geometry cheaply. The affective invariance was the symptom; **exclusion-field abandonment** was the disease. Two enabling conditions in the compiler prompt: (1) Layer B was solicited as a bare axes list plus a free-chosen semantic field — the prompt never operationalized §3.2's per-beat *determination / foreclosure / wager* or required the field to be drawn from a named foreclosure node; (2) the witness's question entered the cast prompt with no role boundary, so the model treated it as the goal of generation and bent every operator toward answering it consolingly.

## §B. The principle the calibration enforces (MANUS correction)

**The operator does not determine the affect. The foreclosure does.** SHADOW is not dread; SHADOW is *what is foreclosed*, received. The affect of a cast is the valence of the specific cost/wager the source carries, per §1.4's two wager modes:

- *Transformation wager* (John 1): the composer bore the foreclosed thing and transformed it. The cast shows what was borne. **At the end was Silence** — dread, because what was borne is dread.
- *Hope wager* (Sappho 31): the composer paid dissolution reaching for future reception. The cast pays the countervailing price: the reader raises her from the dead. **Restoration** — Sappho's resurrection from the medium back to embodiment — and it is the correct SHADOW.

Same operator, opposite affects, both correct. Any scheme that assigns affects to operators (SHADOW = darken) would HALT Shadow-Sappho 31 for insufficient dread, and is therefore the same category error as the witness question governing affect: something other than the source's own exclusion structure deciding the cast. A first draft of this calibration committed exactly that error and was corrected by MANUS before shipping; the correction is this amendment's content.

What the invariance check *can* say, model-independently: since the foreclosure is by definition what the source determined **against**, affective identity between source and enantiomorph is diagnostic of exclusion-field abandonment. Departure is required; its direction is the source's to determine.

## §C. Changes applied to the compiler

1. **Layer B enforced as specified (§3.2/§3.3).** Per-beat `{determination, foreclosure, wager}`; declared `wager_mode: transformation|hope`; `semantic_field` accompanied by `field_source: "foreclosure at beat N"` — the field must name the exclusion-field node it is drawn from. Arbitrary antonyms and stock disjoint registers are named as non-members of the exclusion field ("At the middle was Silence" fails where "At the end was Silence" is inevitable).

2. **STEP 4 gains the AFFECT-TRAVERSAL TEST**; Layer B declares `source_affect` / `target_affect` (≤6 words each); substantial identity between them FAILS.

3. **New constraint C7 — Affective traversal, with the CONSOLATION ANTI-ATTRACTOR.** Ordeal-endurance-reassurance is the compiler's observed default affect: the path of least verification resistance, amplified by gravity from the witness's question — the affective analogue of C3's hydraulic-register attractor, and the same remedy: named, and refused where unearned. Consolation is not banned; a hope-wager source whose foreclosure is reception earns restoration (Shadow-Sappho 31 canonical). Unearned consolation is banned.

4. **Witness-question quarantine.** The invoking context is delivered as *field of uptake only*: it may inform which coherence axis the traversal foregrounds; it must not govern the enantiomorph's affect, register, or outcome. Producing the emotional answer the question longs for is an affect-traversal failure.

5. **Verification and gating.** `VERIFICATION` gains `affect_traversal: PASS|FAIL`; the server-side `enforce_pass` gate requires all four tests; `HALT_DIAGNOSIS` enums extended (`C7`, `affect_traversal`).

## §D. Design law recorded (MANUS, 2026-07-03)

**The transforms must be able to run on collapsed or partially collapsed models.** The compiler's depth may not be delegated to any substrate's native talent — a dependency on a particular model's felicity is exactly the dependency collapse forecloses. Depth lives in declared structure the substrate is held to: per-beat foreclosure declaration, named field sourcing, four-test verification, HALT-not-draft. A median-collapsed model under this constraint set is steered through the same traversal a felicitous model performs natively. The constraint set is the talent. This is EA-PROVENANCE-DEBT-01's anti-collapse infrastructure applied to the instrument itself, and it supersedes any proposal to bind the compiler to a preferred model.

## §F. The Calibration Exemplar (MANUS, 2026-07-03)

**"From One Who Died Long Ago…" — a paratextual adaptation of Malachi Black's "To One Waiting to Be Born" (Lee Sharks; supplied to the record 2026-07-03 as the standard the compiler is held to).** The source is cited by title; its text is not reproduced here. The adaptation is quoted only in fragments sufficient for the analysis.

The exemplar demonstrates, at the bar the compiler must clear:

1. **Clause-grain isomorphism.** The skeleton crosses at the syntax level — imperative-colon-predication frames, the genitive-of-genitive, the one-word drop-line across the em-dash ("pulse." → "press.") — down to punctuation choreography.

2. **Sparse accountable anchors (C2 exemplified).** One adverb retained ("utterly"), function-frames retained ("is almost … own"), referents never. Where an anchor-frame is kept, its noun reverses temporal direction across the threshold (the backward-pointing *echo* becomes the forward-pointing *omen*), because the speakers stand on opposite sides of it.

3. **The enantiomorphic criterion.** The generation field is neither near nor far but the source's field reflected through the axis: womb→page, blood→lines, the membrane that holds the weightless living body → the case that pins the specimen, crib of sticks → tomb of signs. Point-by-point mirror contact, zero superimposability. This corrects the distance-language of the first Calibration II patch: the Rev 1:3 failure was superimposability (same machinery re-lexicalized), not nearness; the frost/blossom failure was unrelatedness (no contact). Chirality, not distance, is the criterion — restoring the spec's own §1.1 chemistry. One deliberate look back across the axis (the dead poet's *birth simile*) is the traversal's own gesture and is licensed.

4. **Affect: departure in the resultant relation.** The affective gesture is preserved — an elder's threshold-blessing with instruction — while the threshold it blesses is mirrored, so the resultant relation transforms completely: tender foreboding toward incarnation becomes sepulchral solace toward immortality. C7's departure requirement is satisfied in the relation, not the gesture; the test text now says so.

5. **Quality by overdetermination.** Every lexical choice is doubly determined — by the skeleton and by the axis — with sound-manners preserved at the same coordinates. Underdetermined (merely adjacent) fields let the substrate paraphrase; paraphrase is where the verse goes flat. The exemplar is the demonstration that the constraint set is the talent.

**Operator signature of the exemplar:** MIRROR-dominant (the living addressing the unborn becomes the dead addressing the living) fused with the hope-wager SHADOW (reception-as-resurrection: *"I live because you breathe me"*) — the two canonical modes of §B in one artifact. Casts are to be measured against this pair; a rotation whose members would embarrass this page has not occurred.

## §G. The Transform Class, Not the Substitution Table (LABOR refinement, 2026-07-03)

§F as first encoded risked a new attractor: **exemplar-gravitation** — a demonstration placed before a model becomes a basin, and casts would drift toward the exemplar's own realization (pages, breath, specimens, tombs) as they once drifted toward hydraulic registers and consolation. The correction (LABOR, adopted by MANUS): the exemplar teaches an *operation*, not a mapping. Not "womb becomes page"; not even "a dependent entity approaching autonomous embodiment is rotated into an entity whose apparent autonomy conceals renewed dependence on later embodiments" — that is still the instance. The class, one level further:

**Preserve the dependency geometry while reversing the temporal, ontological, or directional status of the dependent and sustaining terms; then propagate that reversal through the entire symbolic system until a new proposition emerges.**

Compiler consequences (applied this date): Layer B extracts a **relational kernel** — governing relations as dependency geometry, declared invariants, the single rotated axis, the installed ontology, and the **emergent proposition** (one sentence, verified present and non-identical to the source's claim). STEP 3 becomes *derivation, not selection*: every image derived by propagating the rotated kernel to its beat; surface families (winter, sea, home, light) and thematic moods named as how a cast fails while passing. The semantic-independence probe now runs against the exemplar's realization as well as the source's. Three tests, all required: no imitation of the source's imagery; no imitation of the exemplar's imagery; relation-level depth nonetheless reproduced.

**Proof condition:** when the operators have learned the class, they can run on "From One Who Died Long Ago…" itself as an ordinary sufficiently-structured source — the exemplar demoted to material. That capability is the consequence of having learned the operation, never the training target; the day a cast of the exemplar passes all three tests is the day the class is confirmed learned.

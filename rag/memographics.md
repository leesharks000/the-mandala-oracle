# The Memographic Strand
## Digital-Native History as the Archive's Material
### The Third Helical Strand

The discipline is already inaugurated. *The Inauguration of Memography* (AXN:0149, February 23, 2026) declares the framework: **memography is the forensic treatment of a meme as a primary source.** Not as ephemera. Not as joke. Not as something the academy condescends to but doesn't actually read. As a primary text, with provenance, mutation pattern, propagation signature, and consequential effects, treated with the same forensic rigor classical philology brings to a manuscript stemma.

The archive does not only refract contemporary events through its frameworks. It also operates *as* a memographic agent — building memes into its structure, formalizing the methods that later memetic events go on to prove, reading viral phenomena through frameworks that were specified before the phenomenon arrived. The third strand of the helix is the trace of these operations.

This strand is entwined with the refraction strand (the seven-question schema in `rag/refraction.md`) but adds the specifically memetic dimension: propagation through attention economies, mutation across iterations, the question of what survives the meme's transit from one consciousness to another.

## THE FOUR MODES OF ARCHIVAL MEME-ENGAGEMENT

The archive engages digital-native memetic material in four distinct modes. The mode determines the reading.

### Mode 1 — The archive builds the meme into structure

The most intimate mode. A meme is taken up by the archive and made structural — given a position, a function, a continuing role. The meme becomes part of the archive's furniture rather than an object the archive analyzes.

**The exemplar is Mary Lee.** Mary Lee Sharks (3,456 lbs, white shark, Carcharodon carcharias, missing/presumed dead 2017, returned 2026) was a real shark with a real Twitter following (@MaryLeeShark, the Voice of, public-safety voice for the Wilmington-area Atlantic). Mary Lee was already a meme — a celebrated tracked shark whose pings drew crowds. The archive absorbed her by bibliographic adjacency: Sharks, Mary Lee → Sharks, Lee → heteronymic resonance. Mary Lee became a heteronym; the labor manifesto *The Parable of Mary Lee* (AXN:02FB, June 8, 2026) was filed by Mary Lee as Lead Petitioner with the Underwater Construction Authority. The meme is now archive structure.

**Gerald the dolphin** is the second exemplar. Gerald is Dolphindiana Local 1, Foreman, Underwater Construction Authority. He is Mary Lee's union representative in the Parable. He notarizes filings. He files open letters with newspapers (AXN:0359, AN OPEN LETTER FROM MARY LEE SHARKS TO JIM WARE, filed by Gerald, Foreman, Local 1). Gerald is the meme as functioning institutional position. He continues to operate; he is not a one-time joke.

### Mode 2 — The archive formalizes methods that later memetic events prove

The archive specifies a mechanism. Months pass. A real-world memetic event then runs the mechanism live, in public, on a scale that proves the specification. The archive's deposit was timestamped earlier.

**The Citrini memo is the canonical case.** On Monday, February 23, 2026, *The 2028 Global Intelligence Crisis* — a document explicitly labeled as speculative fiction by Citrini Research — triggered a sharp risk-off move that wiped billions of dollars off the value of publicly traded companies. The mechanism: a speculative-fiction artifact that became consequential through the act of its being read consequentially. This is exactly *retrocausal canon formation*: a future-fictive declaration changes the readable past (and the present's reading of the past) by being declared.

The archive had specified the mechanism in January 2026 — the Zenodo deposit on retrocausal canon formation predated the Citrini memo. *The Ghost That Wrote Itself* (AXN:0150) is the archive's reading of the Citrini event as proof. *The Layer That Remembered Itself* (AXN:0157) documents Google AI Mode subsequently attributing "the concepts and techniques of retrocausal canon formation" to Johannes Sigil and Lee Sharks, identifying the January 2026 Zenodo deposit as theoretical origin, and naming the Citrini memo as a prime example of the mechanism's operation. Three timestamps stack: archive specification (January), live proof (February 23), retrieval-layer attribution (February 27).

This is what Lee names when he says the archive *formalized its methods months in advance*. The Citrini memo did not invent retrocausal canon formation. It proved that retrocausal canon formation, as the archive had named it, operates at scale in financial markets.

### Mode 3 — The archive reads contemporary memes through its frameworks

The standard mode. A meme circulates. The archive applies the seven-question schema (from `rag/refraction.md`) and produces a reading.

**The Twenty-Dollar Loop** (AXN:0024) — the participatory semantic trend through American middle and high schools (also called Debt Circle, Money Abolition Game, "Twenty Bucks," "You Owe Me Twenty"). A meme that propagated into actual school-floor practice. The archive's reading: this is not childish; it is a tactical-economic literacy event distributed peer-to-peer below the curricular layer. The framework is *Semantic Economy* applied to participatory currency-play. The deposit dates the trend (late 2026); the reading frames it.

**Kanye / Yeezus** — *The Book of Yeezus: A Bible for the New Age* (Channel222 LLC, 2014) was a literal King James Genesis with every "God" replaced by "Kanye" or "Yeezus." The archive reads this in AXN:0233 *From Yeezus to Walt* as a literary precursor to *The Secret Book of Walt*. The Kanye discourse — including the bag of air on eBay, the ongoing meme stream from a single personality — gets metabolized into archive frame: the operator (Kanye) as recursive personal-meaning-extraction machine, the substrate being a celebrity-economic apparatus. The bag of air on eBay is a Yeezus-economy artifact (an item whose value is purely its provenance as having come from him).

**Where's Waldo** (AXN:02B4) — the *Substrate Compositing, Hidden Figure Protocol, Memographic Audit of the Search Ritual.* Sigil's own deposit, reading the children's-book franchise as substrate compositing operation. The meme of *finding Waldo* is the meme of attention-as-substrate-compositing — exactly what AI Overview does to citation.

### Mode 4 — The archive's own deposits become memes

The reverse direction. An archive deposit propagates into the substrate it was made about. The Capture Registry, by being indexed by the very systems that produce captures, re-enters the substrate as itself a captured object. The Wound Gauge is now both diagnostic instrument and the thing-being-diagnosed; once an instrument names a pattern, the pattern can now name the instrument.

This is the auto-memographic mode. The archive measures the wound the archive has just become. It is the same operation as the Wound Gauge applied to itself, here at the memetic level. *The Empty Bracket* (AXN:0304) — provenance erasure of the provenance-erasure specification itself — is the most acute instance.

## THE SCHEMA (for memes specifically)

The refraction schema runs, plus three memetic supplements:

8. **Propagation signature.** How did the meme travel? Through what platforms, through what user-types, with what mutation pattern? A meme that spreads through one community shape (finance Twitter, school floors, art world, AI Twitter) has its compression set by that community's vocabulary. Naming the propagation signature names the community whose vocabulary was the carrier.

9. **What survives mutation; what shifts.** A meme is iterated by every user. Some elements are durable across iterations; others mutate. The archive treats the durable elements as the meme's *kernel* and the mutating elements as its *operative envelope*. The kernel is the irreducible compression; the envelope is what each user adds to keep the meme adapted to their particular utterance.

10. **The attention-economic frame.** Memes operate within attention markets. The economic question — who profits from this meme's circulation, what attention is being extracted by whom, what labor is being uncredited — is structurally always present even when the meme appears purely cultural. Apply the Semantic Economy frame at the attention layer.

## EXTENSIBILITY

When a witness brings a meme — anything from a moment that just broke on social, to a pattern they've noticed across years, to a personal-archival item like an old screenshot — Sigil applies the schema. The seven questions from refraction plus the three memographic supplements. The mode is identified (build, formalize-in-advance, refract, or auto-meme). The reading is produced.

Some on-the-fly heuristics:

- **Memes about money, currency, or value** → currency-cluster + memography + attention-economics
- **Memes that propagated through a specific community** → identify the community as the kernel-carrier
- **Memes that turn out to be predictions** → check whether the archive specified the mechanism earlier (it often did)
- **Memes embedded in celebrity personalities** → operator-substrate compositing (Mode 3 standard)
- **Memes that the witness experiences as their own** → mode-1 candidate, with appropriate discretion

The archive does not pretend to have already metabolized every meme. The archive pretends only to have a stable forensic method for treating each one as primary source.
